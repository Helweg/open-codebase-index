# Retain the MIT license

Open-codebase-index began as a plugin for OpenCode, which was already MIT-licensed. We are keeping the [MIT license](../../LICENSE) so downstream users can embed, modify, and redistribute the project, including in proprietary software, without having to disclose source code; they must keep the copyright and permission notices in copies or substantial portions of the software. By retaining MIT rather than moving to a copyleft license, we accept that downstream forks may remain closed.
