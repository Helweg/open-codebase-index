# Post-fix cross-repository agent study, 2026-09-24

**Outcome: improved tool uptake, but no established accuracy superiority.** This exploratory matched trial compared the default OpenCode 1.18.32 `build` agent with and without a locally built OCBI release candidate (`df894a2`, plugin SHA-256 `32f15ff4da573e2e04ea02af896ab5d6d438822e3840766147043d1effccc540`) on four previously unseen read-only, two-repository questions. Treatment called `codebase_context` in **three of four** tasks, versus zero OCBI calls in controls. Both arms passed all four answers at the frozen >=9/12 threshold. Strictly adjudicated blind scores were three ties and one +2 treatment gain, not the preregistered two gains needed for an exploratory positive accuracy signal.

## Protocol and validity

Two tasks traced Mem0 `f14132db443bd721e24b11758c61c04751e518b6` against its lockfile-matched Axios v1.8.4 `9f6f97bcfb7c510103dfcb05641ae8e8f5c08bcc`; two traced Kiro `577dc40ff6ad6d0684ed2c06902989cdb325294a` against Express 5.2.1 `2cd372e34cd6613f4d00836c2ee122f28bddfcb3`. The path-free solver prompts, separate source-backed 12-point rubric, and broad index scope were independently reviewed and frozen before model calls (SHA-256 respectively `aed7bac0ac54f4ff5b4103e885102f19639753b644f9124037063514c5b80796`, `dc514d170a5c9bd93ec504b2d7b83540d666d279cc8d9c0a1ed4398e41f74259`, and `e86e13ac91f58cefdaefc27a50c643ddb693d237fd5c5951fb501bc5795f2ba8`). The four-task manifest hash was `91a93283f5f28773f89b51fcc41830dcb8d87c039a9118e63a5c8f3c91f7549f`. The candidate was **not a published package**.

Both arms used the same pinned, clean Git sources, prompt, Go DeepSeek V4.1 Flash model, default agent, isolated HOME/XDG, and 480-second hard per-arm deadline. Each fresh trial clone was indexed with local Ollama before inference, including its sibling repository. Actual host `index_status` and source retrieval passed for both repositories. Authenticated OpenCode preflights showed matching built-in tools, no control plugin, and exactly the candidate plugin plus 20 OCBI tools in treatment. The citation/cleanliness verifier established answer presence and unmodified source, **not factual accuracy**. Declared 50-call, 300k-token, and $0.10 budgets were polling limits rather than strictly enforced per request; the 480-second deadline was hard.

The first execution attempt was interrupted by the supervising shell's ten-minute wrapper limit before an aggregate result. Its partial artifacts were not scored. That deviation was recorded before a fresh detached execution with the **unchanged manifest, model, adapter, rubric and seed** and a separate V2 artifact directory. V2 completed all eight arms. An independent blinder extracted eight untruncated terminal answers into anonymous private files and kept the arm mapping separate. Two graders checked pinned source and rubric independently before mapping was opened. The sole 2-point scoring disagreement concerned whether one control answer explicitly explained the dynamic `/health` timestamp; on strict rubric interpretation both graders accepted 10/12. No critical cap applied.

## Paired observations

| Task | Control /12 | OCBI /12 | Delta | OCBI calls | Agent seconds, control / OCBI |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Mem0/Axios 01 | 10 | 10 | 0 | `codebase_context` 1 | 79 / 89 |
| Mem0/Axios 02 | 10 | 10 | 0 | `codebase_context` 1 | 71 / 81 |
| Kiro/Express 03 | 10 | 10 | 0 | 0 | 37 / 71 |
| Kiro/Express 04 | 10 | 12 | +2 | `codebase_context` 1 | 113 / 113 |

All eight runs produced source-cited final answers and passed the >=9/12 threshold, so the paired threshold-success difference is zero, with exact two-sided sign-test p = 1. The score gain in task 04 is **not attributable solely to retrieval** from this experiment; its answer also used ordinary read/search tools. Across the four tasks the sum of adjudicated scores was 40/48 control and 42/48 OCBI. This meets the tool-uptake aim but **does not meet** the preregistered exploratory accuracy rule of at least two pairwise wins with no material regression. Four tasks in two correlated repository families cannot establish superiority.

Reported Go model cost was approximately **$0.04766 control versus $0.06201 treatment**, with aggregate agent execution about **282 versus 283 seconds**, respectively. These totals exclude index preparation and are not stable performance estimates. An independent preparatory set of four target and sibling indexes took about 115 and 82 seconds respectively; the trial preparation hook also indexed each fresh arm separately, so its additional cold-start work must not be silently included in the model-time comparison. The model audit emitted only sanitized tool names/counts and final text, not retrieval query/result payloads. We can confirm completed `codebase_context` calls, but cannot assess evidence quality or prove that the retrieved passages caused the score difference.

**Conclusion:** The routing change moved real-agent OCBI use from the earlier mostly nonuse pattern to 3/4 here, while blind answer accuracy remained near a ceiling. Follow-up should test more independently pinned repository families with harder concealed seams and retain redacted retrieval evidence; report cold-index cost separately. Do not claim a general accuracy, speed, or cost gain, and do not publish this local candidate on these results alone. Private answers, audit files, and arm mapping remain owner-only outside the repository.
